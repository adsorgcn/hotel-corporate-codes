## Quick Start

Visit **[hotelcorporatecodes.com](https://hotelcorporatecodes.com)** → pick your hotel chain → click **Book →** next to any code → the hotel website opens with the code pre-filled. Compare the corporate rate against the public rate and book if it's lower.

Or ask our **[AI Travel Advisor](https://hotelcorporatecodes.com)** — tell it your destination and hotel preference, and it recommends the best code for you.

**Data notes** — Codes are community-sourced reference records. Discount ranges and ID-check notes are historical reports from travellers, not guarantees; rates and verification practices vary by hotel and change over time. Entries with a `source` field link to the official hotel page that publishes the code. Machine-readable, evidence-graded releases are published at [github.com/adsorgcn/hotel-corporate-codes/releases](https://github.com/adsorgcn/hotel-corporate-codes/releases) (tags `data-v1.0.N`, built from the `hotel-data-maintenance` branch).

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Average Room Rate Theory** — The bigger the gap between your corporate rate and the hotel's ADR, the more likely they verify ID. Tier-1 cities = more checks.

**Always Compare** — Corporate rates aren't always cheapest. Weekend promotions and flash sales can beat them. Always compare before booking.

**Full Loyalty Points** — Corporate rate bookings earn full loyalty points and count toward elite status at all major chains. This is confirmed policy, not a loophole.

**If Asked for ID** — Worst case: you pay the regular rate. No penalty, no blacklist.
